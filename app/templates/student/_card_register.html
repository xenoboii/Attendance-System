How it works
  1. Only one student can register at a time. If someone else is using the reader, wait a moment.
  2. You have 60 seconds to tap, then 60 seconds to confirm.
  3. Classmates who already have cards can still tap for attendance while you register.
  4. Lost your card? Ask the admin to remove it, then register a new one here.
